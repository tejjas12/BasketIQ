Rule Metrics Overview

Evaluation Framework

Mathematical measures used to evaluate association rules

S

Support

Fraction of all transactions containing the itemset.

Support(A → B) = P(A ∪ B)
C

Confidence

Conditional probability of B appearing when A is present.

Confidence = Support(A ∪ B) / Support(A)
L

Lift

Compares observed co-occurrence against independence.

Lift = Confidence(A → B) / Support(B)
Analytical Interpretation

Lift greater than 1 indicates a positive association, lift equal to 1 indicates independence, and lift below 1 indicates a negative association. Support and confidence should also be considered before interpreting a rule. The discovered associations are descriptive patterns, not causal conclusions.
