How Does Apriori Work?

The foundation of Market Basket Analysis

Apriori Algorithm

Apriori is an Unsupervised Machine Learning algorithm used for Association Rule Mining. It discovers frequently purchased product combinations from transaction data. It follows the Apriori principle: if an itemset is frequent, all its non-empty subsets must also be frequent.

01. Support

Measures how frequently a product combination appears across all transactions.

Support(A → B)
= Count(A ∪ B) / Total Transactions
Higher support means the product combination appears in a larger proportion of baskets.

02. Confidence

Measures how often product B appears in transactions that already contain product A.

Confidence(A → B)
= Support(A ∪ B) / Support(A)
Example: If confidence is 60%, 60% of transactions containing A also contain B.

03. Lift

Measures the strength of association between A and B compared with their independent occurrence.

Lift(A → B)
= Confidence(A → B) / Support(B)
Lift helps identify whether two products are positively associated, independent or negatively associated.
How to Interpret Lift Values
Lift > 1 · Positive Association
Lift = 1 · Independent Occurrence
Lift < 1 · Negative Association
Example: A rule Bread → Butter describes a directional association. Support measures how often both occur, Confidence measures how often Butter occurs when Bread is present, and Lift compares this relationship with Butter's overall frequency. Association does not prove causation.

Research Note

Transaction baskets are derived by grouping purchases by customer and date. Therefore, multiple purchases made by the same customer on the same date are represented as one basket. Association rules describe patterns in historical data and do not guarantee future purchases.
